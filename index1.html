The strategic question

Exhibit 01

Not "how do we make ordering faster." Everyone is already racing on that axis.

Open the Zomato app to actually order something, and the wit disappears. The browsing and ordering flow reads like any other delivery app: fast, functional, and pretty forgettable. That isn't just a branding inconsistency. It's a strategic one.

Zomato's food delivery business depends on session frequency and dwell time, not just order volume. Those two things shape how much ad inventory the platform can sell, how sticky a subscription like Zomato Gold becomes, and how much growth comes from word of mouth instead of paid acquisition. A product with no distinct voice gives people no reason to open it except to transact, so every session stays purely transactional. That's efficient, but it puts a hard ceiling on how much value each visit can create beyond the order itself.

The real question is what Zomato already owns that competitors can't quickly copy, and whether that thing shows up anywhere in the product.

The idea, and why it should work

Exhibit 03

The Food Story Layer is a thin content layer woven into moments people already pass through: browsing, waiting, reordering. No new tab, no new habit.

Waiting

Occupied time feels shorter

Service design has known this for years: idle waiting feels longer and worse than occupied waiting, whether it's an airport baggage claim or a theme park queue. A delivery wait story turns dead time, and the low-level anxiety that fills it, into a small moment of engagement.

Browsing

Context reduces choice paralysis

A two-line story next to a regional dish gives people a reason to try something outside their usual order. That nudges basket diversity, not just basket size.

Reordering

Repetition becomes identity

A repeat order backed by a story stops feeling like a habit and starts feeling like a small act of taste, the kind of fact people forward in a group chat. That turns into an organic distribution loop, not just a retention one.

The business case

Exhibit 05

Zomato's revenue mix runs on more than order volume. Commission and delivery fees sit alongside advertising and subscription revenue, and both of those scale with engagement, not just transactions.

01. Ad inventory

More sessions, more inventory

Advertising is a high margin, engagement driven line in Zomato's revenue mix. Non-transactional opens, meaning people checking the app between orders, are additional ad-eligible sessions the story layer is designed to create.

02. Subscription retention

A reason to keep Gold, beyond the discount

A subscription like Zomato Gold is easy to cancel the moment a cheaper option shows up, if price is the only reason someone kept it. A layer that gives people a reason to open the app for something other than a discount makes that habit harder to break.

03. Acquisition cost

Shareable facts are a free distribution channel

A two-line, forwardable food fact costs nothing to distribute, and it travels through group chats and social screenshots: the same channel Zomato's marketing already wins on, now generated by the product itself.

04. Brand coherence

One less gap for marketing to paper over

Right now, all of Zomato's personality lives in its marketing, so every ad has to do double duty: earn attention and carry the brand's whole voice on its own. If the product sounded like the brand too, marketing wouldn't have to work as hard to make that impression, and every rupee spent on it would go further.

How we'd validate it

Exhibit 07

Before this ships broadly, it should survive a real test, not just a good pitch.

01

Define the leading indicators

Story open rate, time-in-app during delivery wait, non-transactional opens per week, and reorder rate on dishes with stories attached.

02

Run a 4–6 week A/B test

Treatment cohort sees the story layer across browse, wait, and reorder; control sees the current experience. Primary metric: sessions per active user per week.

03

Set a kill criterion up front

If the treatment cohort shows no lift in sessions or reorder rate after six weeks, the layer gets cut before it becomes permanent surface area to maintain.

04

Check for cannibalization

Confirm the layer isn't just moving attention away from checkout. Order completion rate and time to order both need to hold steady.
